About Esti.AI Lab

Transforming Education Through AI Innovation

Esti.AI Lab is a UAE-based educational technology company dedicated to transforming how institutions leverage artificial intelligence and advanced technology to enhance learning outcomes.

Founded on the belief that AI should empower educators and students, we develop intelligent solutions that address real challenges in modern education. From AI-powered tutoring systems to intelligent administrative platforms, our products are designed with educators and learners in mind.

Our team brings together expertise in artificial intelligence, software engineering, educational psychology, and institutional management. We work closely with educational leaders across the UAE and MENA region to create solutions that truly make a difference.
